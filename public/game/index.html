Game Board

Five categories. Twenty-five tiles. One board. Teams take turns picking a tile, and whatever's behind it decides how you win. Some tiles test what you know, some test how fast you think, and some test how fast you run.

How the Board Works

The board has 5 categories across the top. Each category has 5 tiles, worth 10 to 50 points. Harder tiles are worth more. Once a tile is played it greys out, so you always know what's left.

Buzzer Rules

  • When a clue appears, buzz in if you know the answer.
  • The first team to buzz gets to answer.
  • Once your team buzzes, you have 15 seconds to answer.
  • One buzz per team per tile. Once your team buzzes, you've used your chance on that tile, whether you're right or wrong.
  • If you get it wrong, the other teams can still buzz.
  • The host's decision is final.

The Categories

Cryptic Crossy

Guess the Word

A cryptic clue appears on screen. The answer is always a single word. Crack it, buzz, and answer. Each tile has a 3-minute timer. When it runs out, the answer is revealed.

2Buzzer

Picture Abhi Baaki Hai

Guess the Movie from the Plot

A movie's plot appears on screen, without the title. Read it, figure out the movie, and buzz before anyone else. Each tile has a 3-minute timer. When it runs out, the answer is revealed.

3No buzzer

Jagga Jasoos

Fetch the Object

No buzzers here. A tile flips to show an object. Find that exact object somewhere within 91springboard and bring it to the host. The first team to hand it over wins the points. Run.

4Buzzer

Aaja Nachle

Hook Step

Before each song, every team sends up one player who hasn't come up yet. A song starts playing. Buzz in and show the host the song's signature hook step. You're judged on how well you dance, how quickly you pick up the step, and whether you keep dancing for the entire clip. The host decides who nailed it.

5Buzzer · Timed

Main Kaun?

Guess the Character

These tiles are numbered 1 to 5 instead of showing points. Clues appear one at a time: the first clue gets 15 seconds, then a new clue arrives every 30 seconds. The clock pauses while a team answers. The sooner you guess, the more you score: 50 points on the first clue, down to 10 on the last. You still only get one buzz per tile, so don't guess too early.
